# Brand: AI, Actually (YouTube channel)

Plain-English AI tutorials. Calm, clear, modern.

## Colors
- Background: #0E1218 (near black) with a soft teal glow #1A4240 on the right
- Main accent: #41D6B1 (mint)
- Highlight: #FDC45F (amber)
- Extra: #7DC6FF (blue), #C6A0FA (purple)
- Text: #F0F3FB
- Muted text: #8C96A4
- Cards: #1B252F with a 6 px colored bar on the left

## Fonts (Google Fonts)
- Everything: "Source Sans 3". Headings 900, labels 700 in CAPITALS with letter spacing.

## Layout
- Small mint label above every title, e.g. "MODULE 4" or "HOW IT WORKS".
- Footer bottom-left: "AI, Actually" in muted text.

## Motion
- Cards slide up 40 px and fade in, staggered 8 frames.
- Key words get an amber underline that draws from left to right.
- Calm pacing: hold every card at least 2 seconds.
