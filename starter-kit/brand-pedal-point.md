# Brand: Pedal Point Bike Shop (fictional example)

Small bike repair and rental shop in Oslo. Friendly, fast, local.

## Colors
- Background: #0F1A14 (deep forest green)
- Main accent: #F2B33D (sunny yellow)
- Second accent: #4FD1A5 (mint)
- Text: #F4F1EA (warm white)
- Muted text: #9FB3A8

## Fonts (Google Fonts)
- Headings: "Space Grotesk", weight 700
- Body: "Inter", weight 500

## Shapes
- Rounded corners 24 px. Thick 6 px yellow underline for key words.
- A simple bike-wheel circle can be used as a decoration.

## Motion
- Headlines pop in with a soft spring (no bounce over 10%).
- Prices count up from 0.
- Transitions: a yellow wipe from left to right, 12 frames.

## Voice and words
- Short, warm sentences. "Fixed today. Ride tomorrow."
- Prices: "Tune-up 650 kr", "Puncture repair 200 kr", "Bike rental from 250 kr a day".
- Address line: "Pedal Point · Grünerløkka, Oslo" (fictional shop).
