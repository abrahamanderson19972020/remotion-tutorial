# Brand: Daily Science & Engineering (YouTube channel)

How the world works, explained simply. Calm, clear, curious, precise.

## Colors
- Background: #091126 (deep navy) with a faint blueprint grid (lines #5A8CDC at 9% opacity, every 48 px)
- Main accent: #3FA9FF (electric blue) – diagrams, lines, "ENGINEERING"
- Highlight: #FF8A3D (orange) – the one thing to look at, arrows, forces, the "&"
- Text: #F4F7FC
- Muted text: #96AAC8
- Cards: #0E1E3E with a 2 px border #4678BE

## Fonts (Google Fonts)
- Everything: "Source Sans 3". Titles 900 in CAPITALS, labels 700, body 600.

## Logo
- An orange gear (12 teeth) with a blue atom inside: two orbits crossed at 90°, white electrons, orange nucleus.
- Never use three orbits (looks like another company's logo).

## Style rules
- Blueprint look: thin blue lines, dimension lines with small end ticks, labels in capitals.
- Every diagram has short labels in capitals, e.g. "FAN", "COMPRESSOR".
- Real numbers on screen with units (km/h, °C, kN). Round them and say "about".
- Max 6 words of on-screen text per scene.

## Motion
- Parts slide in and lock into place with a soft spring (no bounce over 8%).
- Arrows draw themselves along their path.
- Air and gas are shown as moving dots (particles) that flow in the right direction.
- Gears and fans turn slowly and continuously.
- Transitions: a blue blueprint wipe, 10 frames.
