# Brand: World in Numbers (YouTube channel)

The world, told with real data. Calm, curious, honest, surprising.

## Colors
- Background: #0B1020 (deep ink), with a very faint dot grid (dots #FFFFFF at 5% opacity, every 40 px)
- Data color: #2EC4B6 (teal) – bars, lines, map fill
- Highlight: #FF6B4A (coral) – the one number or country to look at
- Second highlight: #FFC857 (gold) – years, dates, small badges
- Text: #F5F3EE (warm white)
- Muted text: #9AA3B2
- Cards: #131A2E with a 1 px border #2A3550

## Fonts (Google Fonts)
- Titles and big numbers: "Space Grotesk" 700. Numbers use tabular figures so they don't jump while counting.
- Labels and body text: "Inter" 500 / 600.

## Logo
- A round globe made of 7 horizontal bars of different lengths, like a bar chart shaped as a sphere.
- All bars teal, except the middle one, which is coral and a little longer.
- No meridians, no flags, no text inside the logo.

## Style rules
- Every chart has: a short title, the unit, and a source note at the bottom left in muted text.
- Country names in normal case ("India"), labels in capitals ("POPULATION").
- Big numbers are rounded: "8.1 billion", "1 in 2", "+62%". Add "about" for estimates.
- The highlight color is used for ONE thing per scene.
- Maps use one teal scale from dark (low) to bright (high), never a rainbow.

## Motion
- Bars grow from zero with a soft spring (no bounce over 6%).
- Numbers count up with an ease-out, and land on a rounded value.
- Lines draw themselves from left to right.
- In rankings, bars swap places smoothly; never jump.
- Map countries fade in from dark to their color, west to east.
- Transitions: a quick horizontal "data wipe" of teal bars, 10 frames.
